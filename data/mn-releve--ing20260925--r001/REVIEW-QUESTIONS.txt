Open questions from LLM-assisted first-pass registration (2026-09-25)
======================================================================

This registration was drafted by an LLM curation assistant from the source
delivery (releve_site_export_20241105.csv, DNR releve site metadata_06-2018.xlsx,
and DNR Releve Data Use Agreement_2013.doc) originally uploaded at
data/mn releve/raw/ and moved unchanged into the standard raw/ layout during
this registration. It is a DRAFT. A human curator should resolve the items
below before release_status is advanced past "registered".

1. Data Use Agreement not confirmed executed for this delivery -- HIGH PRIORITY
   raw/ancillary information/DNR Releve Data Use Agreement_2013.doc is a
   blank/unsigned request-form template, not a signed copy specific to this
   2024-11-05 export. The agreement's terms are real and binding if executed
   (masking rare/endangered species locations and identities in any external
   product; no re-sharing with third parties without DNR's written consent;
   required copyright notice and MBS-DNR source acknowledgement in derived
   products). Confirm whether the lab has an executed agreement on file with
   DNR covering this delivery, and treat the release as restricted-use in the
   meantime. packages.license=nap and intellectual_rights documents the
   terms, but does not confirm they were actually agreed to for this export.

2. Geographic bounding box left blank
   packages.west/east/south/north were left blank, matching how
   mn-peat-inventory handled the same situation. Coordinates in
   releve_site_export_20241105.csv (utm_easting/utm_northing, and the
   PostGIS the_geom column, decoded as SRID 26915) are NAD83 UTM Zone 15N,
   not WGS84 decimal degrees as the registry dictionary's west/east/south/
   north fields require. No coordinate-transform library (pyproj, GDAL,
   etc.) was available in this environment to perform a real transform.
   A follow-up pass with proper GIS tooling should populate these fields.

3. 157 unique lead surveyors -- not resolved to person parties
   The lead_surveyors_name column names 157 distinct individuals (top
   contributor: Nancy P. Sather, 1,108 of 11,493 records; also George-Ann
   Maxson, Michael D. Lee, Chel E. Anderson, Scott C. Zager, and others with
   hundreds of records each). Unlike mn-peat-inventory's Koochiching County
   report (which gave 18 individuals corroborating title-page/appendix
   context), no external document here names or contextualizes individual
   surveyors -- only the bare CSV column. This was judged insufficient
   evidence to mint 157 person-party records in a first-pass registration.
   If per-surveyor credit matters to the lab, this warrants a dedicated
   contributor-resolution pass, likely starting with the highest-volume
   names.

4. ~14 other contributing institutions -- not registered or credited
   institution_name in the delivered records names 15 distinct institutions
   contributing non-trivial numbers of plots beyond MBS-DNR: USFS-Chippewa
   NF (1,352), Univ. of Minnesota (1,115), NHP-DNR (712), Consultant (448),
   The Nature Conservancy (392), USFS-Superior NF (238), Other (217),
   National Park Service (172), Nat. Res. Res. Inst. (138), Environ. Qual.
   Bd - CuNi (81), Three Rivers Park District (68), DNR-EWR (52), Pollution
   Control Agency (50), plus a smaller remainder. Only mn-dnr (covering
   MBS-DNR, the single largest contributor and the party the Data Use
   Agreement requires be acknowledged) was credited with a party-role in
   this pass. Confirm whether the other institutions should also receive
   credit/investigation roles, and whether any need distinct party records
   from mn-dnr (e.g., is "NHP-DNR" -- the DNR Natural Heritage Program --
   the same party as mn-dnr, or a sub-unit worth its own record?).

5. Amy Kendig's role is inferred from indirect evidence
   registry--parties.csv includes a-kendig based solely on being listed as
   cp:lastModifiedBy of the metadata xlsx on 2024-11-05, the same calendar
   date as the CSV's own download_date. This suggests but does not confirm
   she prepared or delivered this specific export (she may have simply
   edited the metadata document for an unrelated reason on that date).
   Confirm her actual role, or remove the party-role assertion if
   unsupported.

6. Placeholder-date suspicion in date_ column
   coverage_start=1964-01-01 is the literal minimum of the date_ column, but
   258 of 11,493 records (2.2%) share that exact date, and 18 more share
   1977-01-01. A cluster this large sharing January 1 of a round year is
   more consistent with a placeholder/unknown-date data-entry convention
   than 258 independently confirmed field-survey dates on the same day.
   NOT corrected here -- coverage_start reflects the raw data as delivered.
   Confirm with DNR whether 1964-01-01 (and similar January-1 dates) are
   placeholder values, and if so, whether coverage_start should instead
   reflect the next-earliest plausible date.

7. releve_status_name -- most records are "Transfer in Progress"
   8,108 of 11,493 records (70.6%) have releve_status_name = "Transfer in
   Progress" rather than "Quality Checked Complete" (3,285) or the fully
   finalized "Publishable" (3). Per the metadata dictionary, "Transfer in
   Progress" means "Relevé data entered in previous version of MNDNR
   Relevé Database and transferred electronically to new system" -- i.e.
   a system-migration status, not necessarily an indicator of QC/
   completeness. Confirm whether this dataset should be flagged as
   partially provisional, or whether "Transfer in Progress" records are
   otherwise considered final/usable.

8. source_citation constructed, not verbatim
   No "cite this data as ..." string was supplied. The source_citation
   field was built from the Data Use Agreement's acknowledgement
   requirement plus file-metadata evidence. Confirm against DNR's actual
   preferred citation format if one exists.

9. Geographic scope: Wisconsin + missing state_code records
   2 of 11,493 records have state_code=WI (Wisconsin) and 2,443 have
   state_code=NA (missing/blank). geo_description notes this but the
   packages row does not otherwise characterize or exclude these records.
   Confirm whether the 2 Wisconsin records and ~21% missing-state_code
   records are expected/in-scope for this "Minnesota" dataset naming, or
   whether they should be flagged/filtered in a later harmonization pass.

10. ds_iid uses ASCII "releve" (no accent)
    The source term is "relevé" (with acute accent). ds_iid=mn-releve
    drops the accent for kebab-case/ASCII-safety, consistent with the
    ds_iid pattern constraint (^[a-z0-9]+(-[a-z0-9]+)*$) in
    registry--dictionary.csv. The accented form is preserved in
    title/abstract/notes text fields. Confirm this IID is acceptable.
